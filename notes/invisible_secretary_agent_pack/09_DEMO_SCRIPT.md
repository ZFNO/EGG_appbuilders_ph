# Hackathon Demo Script (2 Minutes)

## Scenario
An SME owner is coordinating a supplier delivery and customer communications.

1. Enter: “Ask the supplier to confirm today's delivery.”
2. Show local intent classification: `supplier_followup`.
3. Show policy gate and routing to the follow-up node.
4. The node prepares a draft message. It does not send automatically.
5. Show the approval card with recipient, message, and consequence.
6. Approve the draft in the demo environment.
7. Show the confirmed result and activity log entry.
8. Enter an ambiguous request such as “Handle the issue with the order.”
9. Show that the router asks a clarification question rather than guessing.
10. Finish by showing the three nodes and the local inference status.

## Demo honesty
If nodes are mocked, state that clearly. Do not present fabricated integrations, metrics, or completed external actions as real.
