# Personas and Daily Journeys

## Persona A: SME owner/operator
The owner is responsible for business continuity while switching between employee issues, customer requests, suppliers, sales, finance, and scheduling.

### Typical daily friction
- Messages and requests arrive from several channels.
- Employee questions interrupt planned work.
- Supplier and customer follow-ups are easy to forget.
- Tasks have unclear ownership or deadlines.
- The owner must decide what is urgent and what can wait.
- Information is fragmented across messaging, email, calendars, spreadsheets, and business tools.

### Core need
The business should keep moving without requiring the owner to make every small coordination decision.

### First use cases
1. Follow up with a supplier who has not confirmed a delivery.
2. Check or propose a schedule change.
3. Triage a customer request and route it to the right queue/person.

## Persona B: Student (future profile; not MVP scope)
A student struggles with scattered deadlines, group coordination, task prioritization, and remembering what to do next. This persona is recorded as a future expansion profile, not part of the SME MVP.

## Daily journey example
1. An event or request arrives.
2. The router identifies intent and extracts allowed parameters.
3. Policy checks urgency, confidence, permissions, and action risk.
4. The relevant node validates its own inputs.
5. The node executes a mock or authorized action.
6. The dashboard records the result.
7. The owner is interrupted only for ambiguity, failure, or required approval.
