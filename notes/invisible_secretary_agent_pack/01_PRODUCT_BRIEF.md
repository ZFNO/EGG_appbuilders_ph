# Product Brief

## Working name
Invisible Secretary (working name; product naming is not finalized).

## Target user
Owner/operator of a small or medium-sized enterprise who coordinates people, customers, suppliers, schedules, and routine operations.

## Problem
The owner becomes the human routing layer for a continuous stream of small tasks and exceptions. They repeatedly decide who should act, what should happen next, and whether to follow up.

## Value proposition
A local-first decision layer that converts routine business events and natural-language requests into appropriate actions, reduces context switching and missed follow-ups, and escalates only when owner input or authorization is required.

## Product promise
“Tell it what needs handling. It routes routine work to the right capability and shows you what happened.”

## User experience principles
- Quiet by default: do not force a chat conversation for every event.
- Visible accountability: every routed action has a status and audit entry.
- Owner control: approvals and permissions are explicit.
- No false claims: simulated integrations must be clearly labeled.
- Local-first: prefer local inference; never silently send private data to a cloud model.
- Predictable: use structured outputs, validation, deterministic rules, and bounded actions.

## Success metrics for the demo
- Correct intent classification on a fixed test set.
- No invalid node names or schema-breaking outputs.
- Correct escalation for ambiguous or disallowed actions.
- End-to-end latency and memory usage on target hardware.
- A user can understand what the system did and why.
